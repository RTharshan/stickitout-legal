Privacy Policy

Stick It Out Uploader · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Stick It Out Uploader ("the App") is a private tool that the creator of the "Stick It Out" channel uses to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account. This page explains what data the App handles.

1. Data the App receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (scope user.info.basic): display name, avatar and open ID of the owner's account, used only to confirm that the right account is signed in.
  • Access tokens from TikTok's Login Kit, used only to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • Upload status returned by TikTok, used to check whether a post succeeded.

2. Data the App does not collect

The App does not collect data from viewers or other TikTok users. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it has no user accounts of its own.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to anyone except TikTok's official API servers.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. The owner can delete them at any time by deleting the local token file, and can revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by the channel owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
